# Pieces

A piece is one thing happening to the dots. Effects are made of them, and there are two kinds.

| Kind | What it does | Written as |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Places the message: `stand` holds it, `scroll-across` carries it over the sign, `tumble` turns each letter end over end. | `{ move: 'tumble' }` |
| Modulator | Changes how the lit dots look while it plays: `flash`, `pulse`, `hue-cycle`, `sparkle`, and filters like `fire`, `glitch` and `neon`. | `{ mod: 'pulse' }` |

A move is written for one slot (`enter`, `active` or `exit`), and `MOVES` says which. Modulators go in any slot. Here a scene carries its own effect: a move for each slot, and a hue cycle spread by column while it holds:

A piece takes `params`, which set its settings by name, how long it plays (`ms`) among them. A move also takes an `order` of its own, staggering its dots, and a modulator a `spread`: an order that shifts its phase across the dots rather than delaying them, so a hue cycle runs as a rainbow when spread by column.

Each piece declares its settings as `params`: plain data giving each number's label, range and default, for a control panel to be built from. `flash` blinks a number of times within a window of each pass, `from` and `to` as shares of it, which is how `hold` flashes three times in the middle third of its hold.

Every move and modulator is in the [gallery](../gallery/#move), with its settings.
