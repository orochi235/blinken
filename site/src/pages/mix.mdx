# On your own mix

Every sign is a [blits](https://michaelbaker.tech/blits/) mix underneath, and `sign.mix` hands it over for cueing voices of your own on `sign.board`'s dots and cells. Going further, the parts `createMarquee` is made of are exported, so a mix of your own can play a program and you can draw it however you like.

<Listing of="live/mix.ts" />

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| `board(cols, rows)` | The dots and the border, as subjects a mix drives. |
| `KIT` | The pose they fold to: `lit` by `max`, `gain` by `mul`, `tint` by color, `hue` as an angle that adds and wraps, and `chroma` and `glow` by `mul`. |
| `play(mix, board, scenes, effects?)` | Cues one pass of a program. |
| `cueLayer` | Cues a layer, looping. |
| `createSign(canvas, board, leds)` | Draws the columns a mix was read into, with the sign's faults, fade and ghosting. |
| `paintBulbs` | Gives the border's bulbs their cells' poses. |

Read the mix's columns every frame and hand them to `createSign`, or read them and draw them some other way.
